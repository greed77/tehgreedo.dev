Privacy Policy - Collection Scanner

1. Information Collection and Use

  • Camera Access: Our app requires access to your device's camera. This access is used solely to scan barcodes within the application.
  • Barcode Data: When a barcode is scanned, the numerical or textual data encoded in the barcode is captured locally on your device.
  • No Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), such as your name, email address, location, or device identifiers.

2. Third-Party Data Transmission

  • API Lookup: To provide product information, the scanned barcode data is transmitted to a third-party application programming interface (API).
  • Data Limitation: Only the raw barcode number/text is sent to this external API. No user or device data accompanies this request.
  • No Other Third Parties: We do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or cloud storage SDKs. No other data leaves your phone.

3. Data Retention and Security

  • Local Storage: If a scanned barcode matches a product via the third-party API, the barcode number and basic product details are saved.
  • Anonymity: This stored product data is kept entirely separate from any personal information. It is never tied to your identity, device ID, or any personally identifiable information (PII).
  • Camera Stream: Camera video streams are processed in real-time and are never recorded, saved, or uploaded.

4. Contact Us

  • Inquiries: For any questions regarding this privacy policy, you can contact us at: appsupport@tehgreedo.dev.
