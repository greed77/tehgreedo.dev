What it does

Collections for anything

Energy drinks, Lego sets, puzzles, snacks, or your own categories. An item can sit in more than one collection.

Scan to add

Scan a barcode to add an item, or enter it by hand. Food and drinks fill in their name and brand from Open Food Facts. Rescanning something you have takes you straight to it.

Rate and log

Log an entry each time you revisit an item, with a half-star rating, notes and a date. See the average rating, last rating and entry count at a glance.

Photos

Attach photos to an item itself, or to a single log entry.

Find anything

Sort and filter by name, rating, date added or last logged, and search one collection or your whole catalog.

Your data stays on your phone

No account and no cloud sync. Back up and restore everything, photos included, as a JSON or ZIP file.

Google Play: closed testing

Collection Scanner is in closed testing on Google Play, ahead of a public release. Testers get updates automatically from the Play Store.

Want to help test it? Email me with the Google account you use on your phone, and I'll add you to the tester list.

Download the APK

Every release is also published as an APK you can install directly. Download the latest from the releases page and open it on your phone.

Your collections live only on your phone, so uninstalling deletes them. To switch between the Play version and the APK, export a backup first, then restore it after reinstalling.
