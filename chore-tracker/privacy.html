Chore Tracker privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026

The short version: Chore Tracker stores what it needs to share your household's chores between your family's phones, and nothing else. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and your data is never sold or used for anything but running the app.

What the app stores

  • Your account: your email address and password, used to sign you in. Your password is handled by Google's sign-in service, Firebase Authentication. We never see or store it ourselves.
  • Your profile: the name you choose to show your family, your email address, and which household you belong to.
  • Your household: its name, its invite code, and who owns it.
  • Your household's chores: what you enter for each one, such as its name, room, notes, how long it takes, how often it's due and who it's assigned to.
  • Your household's history: when each chore was done and who did it, plus any skipped or closed-out cycles.

What it doesn't

Chore Tracker doesn't access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files. It doesn't use advertising IDs, and it doesn't include any analytics, crash-reporting or advertising code.

Who can see it

  • Your household: everyone in your household can see its chores, its history, and the names of its members. Your email address is stored in your profile, which other members' apps are allowed to read, although the app doesn't show it to them.
  • Nobody else: no other users can see your household. Someone can join it only with its invite code, and the owner can replace that code and remove members.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which runs the app's sign-in and database on Google's servers. It's encrypted in transit between your phone and those servers. Google processes it on our behalf; see Privacy and Security in Firebase. We don't share your data with anyone else.

How long it's kept, and deleting it

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time, in the app or by emailing us. How to delete your account explains what's removed and what stays with the rest of your household.

Children

Chore Tracker is meant for families, set up by a parent. A child's account holds only what any account does: an email address, a name and their part of the household's chores. A parent can delete a child's account by signing in as them, or by emailing us.

Open source

Chore Tracker's code is public, so anyone can check exactly what it does: code.reedpeople.com/tehgreedo/android_chore_tracker.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions or requests: android@tehgreedo.dev.
