What it does

Shared with your family

Create a household and invite your family with a six-letter code. Everyone sees the same list, and it updates as soon as someone checks a chore off.

Always know what's due

Every few days, weeks or months, on set days of the week, just when needed, or just once. Overdue and due-today chores come first, colour-coded.

Split the big jobs

Break a chore into parts, like one load of laundry per person, and give each part to someone. The chore is done when every part is.

Who did what

Assign chores, filter to just yours, and look back at any chore's history. Forgot to check something off? Log it for the day it happened.

Flexible when life happens

Skip a week without pretending it was done, pin a chore to the top, or let "Surprise me" pick what to do next.

No ads, no tracking

No ads, no analytics, nothing sold. It's open source, and you can delete your account at any time from Settings.

Google Play: closed testing

Chore Tracker is in closed testing on Google Play, ahead of a public release. Testers get updates automatically from the Play Store.

Want to help test it? Email me with the Google account you use on your phone, and I'll add you to the tester list.

Download the APK

Every release is also published as an APK you can install directly. Download the latest from the releases page and open it on your phone.

The APK is signed differently from the Play version, so to switch from one to the other, uninstall first. Your chores are stored online, so you won't lose anything.
