Delete your Chore Tracker account

You can delete your account and your data at any time, from inside the app or by asking us.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Chore Tracker and sign in.
  2. If you're in a household: tap the ⋮ menu, then Settings, then Delete account at the bottom.
  3. If you aren't in a household: on the screen that asks you to create or join a household, tap Delete account at the bottom.
  4. Enter your password to confirm. If you own a household that has other members, choose who takes it over.

Your account is deleted right away, and you're signed out.

Or ask us to delete it

If you can't use the app, for example because you no longer have the phone, email android@tehgreedo.dev from the email address you signed up with, and say that you want your Chore Tracker account deleted. We'll delete it, and reply to confirm, within 30 days.

  • Email us

What's deleted

  • Your sign-in: your email address and password, and the link to your Google account if you added one.
  • Your profile: your name, and your membership of your household.
  • Your household, if you were its only member: the household, all of its chores, and all of its history.

What stays, if others share your household

A household belongs to everyone in it, so it carries on without you:

  • The household and its chores stay. Chores that were assigned to you become unassigned.
  • If you owned the household, the member you choose becomes its owner.
  • Chores you completed stay in the household's history, so everyone's schedules stay correct. They no longer show your name: they're shown as done by "Someone", and nothing links them back to you.

We don't keep any other copy of your data after deletion.
